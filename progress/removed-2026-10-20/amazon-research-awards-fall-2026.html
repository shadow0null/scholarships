About the awards

The Amazon Research Awards (ARA) — Fall 2026 is Amazon's academic research-funding programme. It is not a student scholarship: it funds principal investigators — full-time faculty at accredited institutions and permanent researchers at eligible NGOs — whose work falls under one of nine Fall 2026 research areas: Agentic AI; AI for Information Security; Amazon Security; Automated Reasoning; AWS Cryptography; Build on Trainium (agentic, recursive improvement of kernels and frameworks); Multilingual and Contextual AI Evaluation; On-Device Multimodal Perception; and Synthetic Data Generation for Multimodal Perception. Awards are structured as one-time unrestricted gifts (no administrative overhead), and each funded project is assigned an Amazon research contact plus training resources such as AWS tutorials and hands-on sessions with Amazon scientists and engineers.

Eligibility

  • Academic faculty: must be a full-time faculty member at an accredited academic institution.
  • NGO researchers: must be a permanent researcher at an NGO with recognised legal status in its home country.
  • Researchers worldwide are encouraged, provided the institution holds official accreditation or legal status in its country.
  • The proposal must address one of the nine Fall 2026 research areas with a clear research question, sound methodology and potential significance.
  • A primary PI may submit only one proposal per call; one co-PI is permitted.
  • An Amazon employee contact is not required to apply.

Benefits

  • Unrestricted funds — up to $80,000 depending on the call (about $70,000 on average for Agentic AI).
  • AWS promotional credits — up to $40,000–$50,000 depending on the call.
  • Amazon research contact assigned to every funded project.
  • Training resources: AWS tutorials and hands-on sessions with Amazon scientists and engineers.

Documents required

  1. Research proposal prepared on the official ARA proposal template (about 4 pages max for most calls, plus appendices)
  2. Budget in USD (no administrative overhead)
  3. Open-source contribution plan and AWS tools to be used (per call requirements)

How to apply

  1. Pick the right callRead the specific Fall 2026 call-for-proposals page on Amazon Science for your research area.
  2. Draft the proposal on the official templateExplain the research question, methodology, expected contributions, impact and fit for the call.
  3. Submit through the official portal before 4 November 2026, 11:59 PM Pacific Time
  4. Decision letters are sent in February 2027.
Not a student scholarship. ARA funds academic research led by faculty or NGO researchers — students cannot apply as PIs. PhD students may participate as part of a faculty-led proposal.

FAQs

Is this a scholarship for students?

No. Amazon Research Awards is a research grant for faculty and NGO researchers. Students cannot apply as principal investigators.

Can researchers from India apply?

Yes — researchers worldwide are encouraged, provided their institution holds official accreditation or legal status in its country.

Do I need an Amazon contact to apply?

No. An Amazon contact is not required; funded projects are assigned one after selection.

How many proposals can I submit?

One per call as primary PI; a co-PI meeting the eligibility rules is allowed.
