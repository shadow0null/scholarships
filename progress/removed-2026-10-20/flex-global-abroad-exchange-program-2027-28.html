About the program

The Future Leaders Exchange (FLEX) Global Abroad Program, funded by the U.S. Department of State and administered by American Councils for International Education, sends American high school students abroad for one academic year. Participants attend a local high school, live with a host family, and join leadership and cultural activities in one of ten countries: Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Morocco, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, and Thailand.

Important for Indian readers: this program is only for U.S. citizens — Indian students cannot apply. We list it because it appears on many scholarship lists with misleading framing; if you are an Indian high-schooler looking for exchange programs, look instead at programs that accept Indian nationals.

Eligibility

  • U.S. citizenship required.
  • Enrolled in grades 9–12 during the application period (homeschooled students eligible).
  • Aged 15 to 18.5 at the start of the program.
  • Willing to attend a local school and live with a host family abroad.

Benefits

  • Round-trip international airfare.
  • Host-family accommodation and meals.
  • Modest monthly living stipend.
  • Host-country visa costs covered.
  • Emergency medical and accident insurance.
  • Local high-school enrollment and in-country support.

Documents required

  1. Online application via the AIS Forms portal
  2. Proof of U.S. citizenship
  3. School enrollment records
  4. Parental consent forms

How to apply

  1. Go to the official FLEX Global Abroad pageamericancouncils.org — FLEX Global Abroad section.
  2. Register on the AIS Forms portalSelect the FLEX Global Abroad program option.
  3. Complete and submit before 30 November 2026

FAQs

Can Indian students apply?

No — U.S. citizenship is a hard requirement.

Which countries can participants go to?

Ten: Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Morocco, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Thailand.

Is it really fully funded?

Yes — airfare, host family, meals, visa, insurance, and a monthly stipend are covered.

Do I need to know the local language?

Previous language study is not required for most programs.
