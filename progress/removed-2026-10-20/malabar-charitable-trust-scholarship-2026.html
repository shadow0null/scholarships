About the scholarship

The Malabar Charitable Trust Scholarship 2026 — the trust's "Empowering Education: Empowering Womenhood" scheme, running since 2007 — supports girl students from government schools to reduce dropouts and keep them studying through higher secondary. The trust reports over 1.45 lakh girl students supported across 18 states and 3 UTs, with more than Rs 103 crore disbursed to date.

Eligibility

  • Female student.
  • Minimum 50% marks in the Class 10 examination.
  • Pursuing higher secondary studies in a government school (aided schools in selected states) — full-time, regular programme. Distance learning and correspondence students are not eligible.
  • Preference to students with the highest marks and the lowest family income.

Benefits

  • One-time financial assistance up to Rs 10,000, graded on the selection score: 60–100 marks → Rs 10,000; 40–55 → Rs 8,000; 25–35 → Rs 6,000.
  • Scoring is on qualifying-exam marks, family income, parental support, disability and chronic illness in the family.
  • Disbursed by RTGS directly to the student's bank account.

Documents required

  1. Class 10 certificate and marksheet (Class 11 marksheet for Class 12 students)
  2. Applicant's photograph
  3. Bank passbook
  4. Declaration form / bonafide certificate
  5. Proof of family income
  6. Disability certificate / death certificate (if applicable)

How to apply

  1. Check eligibility onlineUse the eligibility checker on the trust's official website.
  2. Register and fill the online formUpload scanned documents.
  3. Submit hard copiesTo the nearest Malabar Gold & Diamonds showroom within 3 days of online submission.
  4. Await verification and selectionResults are notified by SMS; the amount is disbursed by RTGS.
Cycle dates vary: different listings cite different deadlines for this scheme's cycles. Confirm the current cycle's last date on the official Malabar Charitable Trust website before applying.

FAQs

How much will I receive?

Up to Rs 10,000 one-time, graded by your selection score (Rs 6,000–10,000).

Who can apply?

Girl students with 50%+ in Class 10, studying higher secondary full-time in government schools.

Can distance-learning students apply?

No — only full-time, regular programmes.

How is the money paid?

By RTGS directly to the selected student's bank account.
